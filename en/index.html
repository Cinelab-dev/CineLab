Your short film, from script to set

CinéLabs is free software for Windows. It breaks down your script, builds the stripboard and fills in your call sheets, with no subscription and no internet needed. The software is in French today; the English version is on its way.

    Your questions

    Why is CinéLabs free?

    Because it was made for low-budget shoots and first films, where every euro counts. It is developed by a volunteer; if you can, a donation helps it grow.

    Is it available in English?

    Not yet. Today the software, its menus and the printed documents are in French. It already reads English scripts (INT./EXT., DAY/NIGHT, Final Draft and Fountain files) and counts pages in eighths. A proper English version, checked by English-speaking crew, is planned. Tell us you are interested and we will let you know when it is out.

    Windows blocks the installer. What should I do?

    The “Windows protected your PC” message appears for recent independent software. Click “More info”, then “Run anyway”. The installer needs no administrator rights.

    Where are my projects stored?

    On your computer, in the folder you choose when installing. Nothing is sent over the internet. CinéLabs makes an automatic backup every day, and you can make one by hand and copy it to a USB stick.

    How do I send a project to the crew?

    Export the project: CinéLabs makes a single .cinelabs file with everything in it, photos and drawings included. On the other computer, open it and it is added next to the other projects. Accounts and passwords are never in that file.

    Do I need an internet connection?

    No. The internet is only used to email call sheets and to check for a new version. Everything else works offline, on set too.

    How do tablets on set connect?

    Turn on tablet access on the computer and choose a password. Tablets and phones on the same wifi scan the QR code shown and open CinéLabs in their browser, with nothing to install. Everyone can have their own account, showing only the parts that concern them.

    Which scripts can I import?

    PDFs (exported from Final Draft, Fade In, WriterDuet, Word…), Final Draft (.fdx) and Fountain files. A scanned PDF (photos of pages) cannot be read: you need the original file.

    How do I update CinéLabs?

    CinéLabs tells you itself when a new version is out. Download it and run it: it replaces the old one and keeps all your projects. What is coming next is on the roadmap (in French).

    I found a problem, or I have an idea.

    Come and talk about it on the CinéLabs Discord (English is welcome) or email us.
